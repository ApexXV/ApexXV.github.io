About AP3X

Sebastien Gifford, founder.

AP3X Operations LLC is a New York single-member LLC, established August 28, 2026 and based in Franklin, NY. It brings together three kinds of work Sebastien has done hands-on: keeping operations organized, keeping technology running, and making useful physical parts.

How the work gets done

Clear, practical and written down.

The common thread across roofing operations, telecom installs, print services and home-lab infrastructure is the same: understand how the work actually happens, make it simpler, and leave it documented for whoever comes next.

Plain-language communication

Patience, clear explanations and attention to detail, especially for people who do not work with technology every day.

Documentation that outlasts the job

Instructions detailed enough that the next person is not left guessing, written at the level the reader actually needs.

Hands-on, practical fixes

Real experience with the hardware, software and paperwork involved, rather than recommendations from a distance.
