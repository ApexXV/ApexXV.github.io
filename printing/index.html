Materials

Chosen for what the part has to do.

The right material depends on load, heat, flexibility and finish. If you are not sure which you need, describe the use and AP3X will recommend one.

PLA and PLA Matte

Everyday parts, organizers, fixtures, tags and signage. Clean finish in gloss or matte.

  • Full spectrum of colors in stock.

PETG and PETG-CF

Tougher parts with more impact and heat resistance than PLA. Carbon-fiber PETG‑CF adds stiffness for brackets, mounts and jigs.

  • Translucent PETG available.

TPU

Flexible parts such as grips, bumpers, covers and protective pieces.

  • AMS-compatible grades for multi-material parts.

ASA

Outdoor and sun-exposed parts. Resists UV and weathering, and handles heat better than PLA or PETG.

  • Printed in the enclosed X1 Carbon.

How a print job works

From file to finished part.

Every job is quoted individually. Price and timing depend on size, material, quantity and how much design work is needed.

Send STL, 3MF or STEP files, or start from a sketch and measurements.

Send the file or the idea

Email a model file, a sketch, photos or measurements. If there is no model yet, describe what the part needs to do and where it fits.

Review and quote

The design is checked for printability, then material, orientation and strength are chosen for the job. You get a quote and an estimated time before anything prints.

Print, inspect, hand off

Parts are printed, cleaned up and checked before handoff, with any notes you need for fit or use.

What AP3X does not print

Safety-critical parts are declined. That includes anything a person's safety depends on, such as load-bearing parts for people, vehicle steering or braking components, pressure-holding parts, and medical devices. Weapons and weapon components are also declined.
